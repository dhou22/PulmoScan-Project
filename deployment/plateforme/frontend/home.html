Transforming Lung Cancer Diagnosis with AI

PulmoScan leverages cutting-edge machine learning and radiomics to assist clinicians in detecting and classifying lung nodules with greater accuracy and speed. From DICOM imaging to intelligent prediction, we streamline the diagnosis pipeline.

Advanced CT Scan Interpretation

Using annotated CT scan datasets, PulmoScan pinpoints pulmonary nodules with precision. Our platform is designed to support radiologists with automated and explainable outputs.

Benign vs Malignant Classification

Beyond detection, PulmoScan predicts the malignancy of detected nodules and distinguishes between lung cancer subtypes, assisting in faster, more informed treatment decisions.

Data-Driven Clinical Insights

With integrated dashboards and patient-level insights, PulmoScan empowers healthcare professionals to monitor, compare, and act on real-time diagnostic predictions.
